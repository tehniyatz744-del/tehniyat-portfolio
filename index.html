THE 7 CHECKS

What I check, point by point

1

Google Search — can patients find you at all?

In simple words: a patient types a treatment on Google. If your clinic is not on the first screen, that patient never knows you exist.
Exact check: I search 3 phrases — "[your main treatment] near me", "[treatment] + [your area]" (e.g. laser hair removal Johar Town), and your clinic's own name. Screenshot 1: the search results page.

2

Maps & Business Profile — are you in the top 3?

In simple words: Google Maps shows only 3 clinics first — the "top 3" get almost all the calls.
Exact check: I open Google Maps, search your treatment + area and note your position. Then I open your profile and check 6 things: pin location, opening hours, phone / WhatsApp number, main category, services list, and number of photos.

3

Website — can a patient book in seconds?

In simple words: if your website is slow or has no clear call button, the patient goes back and picks the next clinic.
Exact check: I open your website on a phone view and check: does it load, is there a Call / WhatsApp button visible without scrolling far, are treatments and prices listed? If you have no website, I mark it as missing — many clinics don't.

4

Instagram / Facebook — does it build trust?

In simple words: after finding you on Google, many patients check your Instagram before booking. An empty or old page creates doubt.
Exact check: date of your last post, whether your bio states your area + a booking / WhatsApp link, and whether you have Highlights (prices, results, clinic tour).

5

Reviews — do patients trust you?

In simple words: a 4.8 rating with only 4 reviews loses against a 4.6 rating with 400 reviews — patients trust the number of reviews.
Exact check: your total reviews, your star rating, date of your newest review, whether you reply to reviews — then the same numbers for the nearest competing clinic. Screenshot 2: your reviews next to that competitor's.

6

Google Ads — is a competitor paying to appear above you?

In simple words: the first results on Google are often ads. If a competitor runs ads on your treatment and you don't, they take the first calls.
Exact check: I search your main treatment + area again and note every ad at the top: which clinics are running them, and whether your clinic is one of them. Screenshot 3: the ads at the top of the results.

7

Your Score out of 10 — the final result

In simple words: points 1–6 combined into one score, so you know exactly where you stand.
You receive: your score /10, your 3 biggest problems in order, and 3 specific fixes — e.g. "add your 8 treatments as services", not vague advice like "improve your SEO".
